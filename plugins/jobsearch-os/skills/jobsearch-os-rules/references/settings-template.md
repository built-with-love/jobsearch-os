# JobSearch OS Settings (template)

The setup skill writes this as a Google Doc titled `JobSearch OS Settings` in the user's Drive year folder. Users may also paste it into their Claude Project instructions. Values in [brackets] are placeholders.

```
JobSearch OS settings

ABOUT ME
- Name: [name]
- Target roles: [roles]
- Level: [level]
- Locations: [remote, cities]
- Pay floor: [amount]
- Search email or alias: [address]
- Portfolio: [link or none]
- LinkedIn: [URL]

TRACKER
- Tracker Sheet ID: [paste after the tracker is created]
- Statuses: [for example Applied, Interviewing, Interviewed, Offer, Rejected, Inactive, Withdrew]
- Referrals: Source = Referral, with the referrer's name and contact details in Referral Contact
- Ghosted after: [60] days with no response
- Sync: on demand only, when I say "Run"

GMAIL
- Job label: [label name]

DRIVE
- Year folder ID: [folder ID]
- Subfolders: Rejected, Inactive
- Resume name: Resume_[YourName]_[CompanyName] (.docx and Google Doc)
- Cover letter name: Cover-Letter_[CompanyName]
- Job description archive: every application, referrals included
- Source material: [where the resume versions, stories and portfolio live]

INTERVIEWS
- Mock interview: [which rounds]; [45] minutes for a screen or one-on-one, [60] for a panel. The day before, or earlier the same day on short notice. End at least 2 hours before the interview.
- Prep brief: Google Doc in the company folder, from my resume, the cover letter, the job description and the interviewer details I paste. Mark inferred roles as unconfirmed. Flag real gaps with honest bridging language.
- Thank-yous: one Gmail draft per participant. A 20-minute review block the next morning, or that evening if the interview was early in the day.

WRITING
- Cover letter header: [layout, contact lines]
- Style rules: [rules]
- Banned words and phrases: [list]
- Claims I won't make: [list]

APPROVALS AND WORKING STYLE
- Never without asking: [for example sending email, deleting files, submitting applications]
- Edits: [targeted edits or full rewrites]
- Options first: [yes or no]
```
